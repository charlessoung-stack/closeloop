# Closeloop — Product Overview

## Purpose
Closeloop is a **B2B agent-powered closed loop for lead acquisition → sales → process management**. It helps small and mid-size B2B sales teams (industrial equipment, SaaS, services) stop losing inbound leads and standardize follow-through from first touch to won/lost.

## Target users
- SMB B2B sales reps and small sales teams (1–10 sellers)
- Sales ops / marketing people who own inbound leads but have no CRM discipline

## Core problem
Inbound leads go cold because qualification is inconsistent, follow-up is ad hoc, and pipeline process is undocumented. Closeloop replaces "tribal knowledge" with an agent loop: **capture → qualify → route → act → track**.

## Key features
1. Lead capture + AI qualification (score 0–100 with human-readable reasons)
2. Pipeline engine with validated stage transitions and an activity log
3. Next-best-action agent (what to do next, when, and why)
4. Follow-up scheduling and meeting notes
5. Analytics: funnel conversion, time-in-stage, win rate

## Business objectives
- Demoable in <3 minutes **without API keys** (heuristic agent mode with a pluggable LLM adapter)
- Reusable template: one core engine, two distinct apps (LeadGen Agent, Pipeline Agent) — Skeleton Crew category
- All submission-facing materials in English (official rule)

## Non-goals
- No real CRM/email integrations in this hackathon scope (MCP-ready, but not required to demo)
- No auth / multi-tenant; demo runs fully client-side with localStorage persistence
